How health is computed. Each station folds its sensors onto the manifold primitive z = x·y: x = supply adequacy (pressure · flow · reserves), y = integrity & quality (no leak/break/freeze · water in spec · sensors trusted). Multiplying is honest — a perfect-pressure station with contaminated water, or a great-quality main with an active leak, is not healthy, so one failing axis collapses the score. z then drives the color spectrum (red→amber→green), the warning lights, and the alarms. The same framework runs every scale in the dropdown; only the station and sensor counts change.
