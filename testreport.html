Geometry / science PROOFS (tests/geometry.test.js)

  • z = x·y — bounded/symmetric/monotonic; a failing axis collapses z (and would-be averaging is shown to be the bug).
  • z = x·y² — quadratic axis ~2× more sensitive; justified by the C(n,2)=n(n−1)/2 = O(n²) combinatorics (doubling size >2× the coupling).
  • Series reliability R = ∏Rᵢ (weakest-link); MTBF → exponential annual failure rate.
  • Schwarz P / D & Gyroid — proven triply periodic and minimal (mean |H|≈0 on the level set), discriminated against a non-minimal sphere (~59×).
